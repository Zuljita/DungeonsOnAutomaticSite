Install it

Foundry installs modules from a manifest URL. Here is this one:

https://assets.dungeonsonautomatic.com/monsters/enraged-eggplant/foundry/module.json
  1. Open the Add-on Modules tab on Foundry's setup screen.
  2. Click Install Module.
  3. Paste the URL into the Manifest URL field at the bottom of the dialog.
  4. Click Install.
  5. Open your world and enable Dungeons on Automatic Monster Library (GURPS) under Game Settings → Manage Modules.

The monsters appear under Compendium Packs as DOA Monsters (GURPS). Drag one onto the canvas.

You need two things first. Foundry VTT v13 or later, and the GURPS Game Aid system. The Game Aid is a free community system for playing GURPS in Foundry. This module is monster data for it, so install that first.

What you get

Every Actor comes from the same library the bestiary and the dungeon generator use.

Art

The portrait sits on the Actor sheet. The token is the flat-top hex cut of the creature, ready for a hex grid.

Stats

Attributes, HP, FP, Basic Speed and Move. Every attack with its skill, damage and reach. The full trait and skill lists.

Armour

DR is set on every hit location, using the body plan that fits the creature. A naga gets the vermiform table. A dragon gets wings and hind legs.

Credit

Each Actor carries a note naming its licence and the people credited for it.

Updates. Installing a newer version updates the monsters already in your world. It will not duplicate them.
