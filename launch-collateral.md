## SJ Games Forums Draft

Subject: Dungeons on Automatic: unofficial dungeon-prep generator for DFRPG/GURPS

Hi folks,

I have been working on Dungeons on Automatic, an unofficial free desktop/table
aid for generating DFRPG/GURPS dungeon prep. It produces deterministic dungeon
layouts, GM and player maps, room-key material, source-aware filtering, and
exports for local prep workflows including Foundry and Universal VTT.

It also ships a free public bestiary: 457 monsters with original portraits,
tokens, complete GURPS stat blocks, and GCS files, browsable in a browser with
nothing to install, and installable into Foundry VTT as a compendium module.

The public site is here:
https://dungeonsonautomatic.com/

Quickstart:
https://dungeonsonautomatic.com/quickstart.html

Bestiary:
https://dungeonsonautomatic.com/monsters.html

Downloads, first-run steps, and known issues:
https://dungeonsonautomatic.com/releases.html#downloads

The project is intended as a prep aid for people using the books they own, not a
replacement for published rules or source-book text. The site carries the
SJ Games notice on each page. The monster library contains only independently
authored records and authorized fan-conversion material with per-record
provenance and credit; no SJ Games text, art, or trade dress is republished.

Bug reports and feedback are welcome on the public tracker:
https://github.com/Zuljita/DungeonsOnAutomaticSite/issues

## Blog Draft

Title: Dungeons on Automatic: public launch notes
Tags: launch, release

Dungeons on Automatic is moving toward its public launch shape: a free,
local-first dungeon prep tool for DFRPG/GURPS tables, plus a free public
bestiary of 457 statted, illustrated monsters. The public site has a focused
quickstart, release downloads, per-platform first-run steps, an honest known
issues list, privacy notes, and a clear source/license boundary for the
monster library.

Start with the quickstart:
https://dungeonsonautomatic.com/quickstart.html

Browse the bestiary:
https://dungeonsonautomatic.com/monsters.html

Then grab a build from the downloads section:
https://dungeonsonautomatic.com/releases.html#downloads

Launch-week expectations:

- Continuous builds are early access; the builds are unsigned and each OS
  challenges the first launch (steps are on the releases page).
- Use the source books you own at the table.
- Player exports are intended to keep GM-only details hidden.
- Universal VTT import hardening is still in progress.
- Bug reports with a seed and app version are the most useful thing you can
  send: https://github.com/Zuljita/DungeonsOnAutomaticSite/issues
