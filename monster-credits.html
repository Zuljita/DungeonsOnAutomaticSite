Somebody did the conversion work.

The bestiary exists because other people published good work under terms that let it be reused, and because they said yes when asked. This page names them, states what the library is licensed under, and marks the line we do not cross.

The conversions

The monster mechanics in this library are a GURPS fan conversion by Enraged Eggplant, from their collection Monsters (11 May 2024). They gave unrestricted permission for the work to be adapted and republished here, and the permission itself is public so you do not have to take our word for it.

Original GURPS conversion by Enraged Eggplant, from Monsters (May 11, 2024); adapted and republished with permission.

Dungeons on Automatic added the review pass, the encounter maths, the art, and the GCS export. Every record carries its own credit line and conversion notes inside the data — open any monster on the bestiary page and expand Where this one came from to read them.

The art

Every portrait, token, and hex token in the bestiary is original art generated for this project with OpenAI image generation, in a single house style so the library looks like one book rather than a scrapbook. Hex tokens are derived from the top-down tokens and cut to each creature's stated footprint.

No publisher art, logo, or trade dress is hosted here or generated to imitate one. The images are yours to download and use with the monsters they belong to.

What is deliberately not here

Dungeons on Automatic is an unofficial game aid. Steve Jackson Games book text, published monster stat blocks, art, logos, and trade dress stay with their publisher and are never republished through this site.

Published monster packages must name their source basis at the record level, and each source family keeps its own provenance category rather than being merged into a vague bucket. Nothing reaches the public library without passing that review.

You should still own and use the books on your shelf. The source-book filtering in the desktop app exists so the generator stays inside the library you actually have.

The data itself

The whole library is one JSON file: monsters, stats, provenance, and asset URLs. If you want to build something on top of it, start here rather than scraping the page.
