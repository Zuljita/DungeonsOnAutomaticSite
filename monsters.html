    Source and IP boundary

    Manual review precedes every package-metadata release. Steve Jackson Games book text, monster stat blocks, art, logos, and trade dress remain with their publisher.

    Public monster packages must identify their source basis at the record level. Each source family retains its own provenance category, and record-level credit lines travel with the package.
