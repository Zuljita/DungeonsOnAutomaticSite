The bestiary

Something is already in the room.

Three hundred–odd monsters, each with an original portrait, a top-down token, a hex token cut to its real footprint, and a complete stat block. Search it by name or by what the thing actually does — petrification, engulf, impaling — then print the block or import it into GCS. Free, no account, nothing to install.

What comes with every entry

Open any monster and you get the whole thing — not a teaser card that sends you shopping.

Art you can drop on a map

A portrait for the reveal, a top-down token for play, and a flat-top hex token sized to the creature's real footprint. A 14-hex aboleth arrives 14 hexes wide. All three download as PNGs.

A stat block, not a summary

Attributes, derived stats, every attack with skill, damage and reach, the full trait and skill lists, and the notes that explain the nasty bit. Hit print and the page hands your printer the block alone.

Ready for GCS and for DOA

Each monster ships as a GCS v5 character sheet and as an ancestry template you can apply to any character. The desktop app draws on this same library when it stocks a dungeon.
