The bestiary

Something is already in the room.

Three hundred–odd monsters, each with an original portrait, a top-down token, a hex token cut to its real footprint, and a complete stat block. Search it by name or by what the thing actually does — petrification, engulf, impaling — then print the block or import it into GCS. Free, no account, nothing to install.

What comes with every entry

Every monster carries the same set of files.

Art

A portrait, a top-down token, and a flat-top hex token cut to the creature's footprint. A 14-hex aboleth arrives 14 hexes wide. All three download as PNGs.

The stat block

Attributes and derived stats, every attack with its skill, damage and reach, the full trait and skill lists, and the notes covering special abilities. Printing a monster's page gives you the block on its own.

GCS files

A GCS v5 character sheet and an ancestry template you can apply to any character. The dungeon generator draws on this same library when it stocks a dungeon.
